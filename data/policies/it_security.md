# IT Security and Hardware Policy

## 1. Password Requirements
Passwords for all corporate accounts must meet the following criteria:
* Minimum of **14 characters**.
* Must include at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
* Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.

## 2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is **mandatory** for all internal systems, including email, Slack, and the VPN. Use of the "Okta Verify" or "Google Authenticator" app is required.

## 3. Physical Device Security
* Laptops must never be left unattended in public spaces or unlocked vehicles.
* All company-issued hardware must be returned immediately upon termination of employment.

## 4. Software Installation
Employees are prohibited from installing unauthorized third-party software on company laptops. Contact IT Support for a list of pre-approved applications.
