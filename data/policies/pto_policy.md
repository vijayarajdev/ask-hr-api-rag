# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## 1. Overview
Our company provides Paid Time Off (PTO) to all full-time employees to ensure a healthy work-life balance.

## 2. Accrual Rates
* **Standard Employees:** 20 days (160 hours) per calendar year.
* **Senior Management (Director+):** 25 days (200 hours) per calendar year.
* PTO is accrued monthly at a rate of 1.66 days per month.

## 3. Carryover Rules
Employees are allowed to carry over a maximum of **5 days** of unused PTO into the following calendar year. Any days exceeding this limit will be forfeited on January 1st.

## 4. Requesting Time Off
Requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least **two weeks** in advance for any leave exceeding three consecutive days.
